After the Gig is live

Rules for the first 30 days

Keep only one main service.
Check messages often. Reply within 24 hours — faster is better.
Do not accept unclear orders.
Use delivery times you can really meet.
Send progress updates.
Deliver early only when the work is truly ready.
Check every file before you send it.
Never buy or fake reviews, orders, or traffic.
Each week write down impressions, clicks, messages, and orders.
Change only one Gig thing at a time.
Practise the actual skill at least one hour every day.
Do not spam strangers with your Gig.

Remember

One competent beginner who can attract, understand, and serve the first client — without avoidable cancellations, fights, or poor reviews.
